Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best Bluetooth speaker for under $50?

The JBL Clip 4 at $49.95 is our top pick under $50. It's IP67 waterproof, has a built-in carabiner for portability, and delivers solid sound. If you want even cheaper, the JBL Go 3 at $29.95 is excellent for the price.

Do more expensive Bluetooth speakers sound better?

Generally yes, but diminishing returns kick in fast. The jump from $30 to $80 is massive (JBL Go 3 to Anker Motion+). The jump from $80 to $150 is noticeable but less dramatic. Our best value pick (Anker Motion+) sounds 80% as good as our best overall for half the price.

What does IP67 waterproofing mean?

IP67 means the speaker is fully protected against dust ingress and can be submerged in 1 meter of water for 30 minutes. IPX7 is similar but without the dust rating. Both mean the speaker handles rain, pool splashes, and beach use without worry.

Can Bluetooth speakers connect to each other for stereo?

Many do — the UE Boom 4 and JBL Charge 5 both support pairing two units for stereo sound. The Anker Motion+ supports this through the Soundcore app. Check each speaker's specs, as this feature varies.
