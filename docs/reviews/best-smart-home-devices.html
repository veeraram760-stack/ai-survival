Frequently Asked Questions

What is Matter and why does it matter?

Matter is a universal smart home standard backed by Apple, Google, Amazon, and Samsung. Matter devices work with any smart home platform — buy a Matter light bulb and it works with HomeKit, Google Home, Alexa, and Samsung SmartThings. It's the end of ecosystem lock-in. Look for the Matter logo when buying new devices.

Do I need a hub for smart home devices?

It depends. WiFi devices (smart plugs, cameras) connect directly to your router — no hub needed. Zigbee and Thread devices need a hub or border router. Most people start with WiFi devices and add a hub when they want Zigbee/Thread lights and sensors. A HomePod Mini or Echo 4th Gen serves as both speaker and Thread hub.

Are smart home devices secure?

Generally yes from major brands (Apple, Google, Amazon, Philips, TP-Link). Use strong unique passwords, enable two-factor authentication where available, keep firmware updated, and put IoT devices on a separate WiFi network if your router supports it. Avoid cheap no-name devices from unverified sellers.
