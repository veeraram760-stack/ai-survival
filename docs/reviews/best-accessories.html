Frequently Asked Questions

Should I get a 4K monitor or ultrawide for productivity?

For most productivity work (documents, coding, browsing), a 27" 4K monitor is the sweet spot — sharp text and enough screen space for split-screen multitasking. Ultrawides (34" 21:9) are better for video editing, creative work, and having multiple full-size windows side by side. If you can only have one monitor, 4K at 27" is the safer choice.

Mechanical or membrane keyboard for office use?

Low-profile mechanical (like the Keychron K3) gives you the typing feel of mechanical without disturbing coworkers. Full-size mechanical switches are great for home offices but too loud for shared spaces. Membrane keyboards are quiet but less satisfying for heavy typists. Our recommendation: low-profile mechanical for the best of both worlds.

How much should I spend on desk accessories?

A complete desk upgrade (monitor + keyboard + charger + cable management + desk mat) runs about $500 with our picks. That's a one-time investment that improves every workday for years. Start with the biggest pain point — usually the monitor or keyboard — and upgrade from there.
