Frequently Asked Questions

Are wireless earbuds as good as wired now?

For most people, yes. Bluetooth 5.3+ with LDAC or AAC codecs delivers sound quality indistinguishable from wired for casual listening. Audiophiles with high-end gear will still notice differences, but for 95% of use cases, wireless is just as good.

How long do wireless earbuds last?

The earbuds themselves typically last 3-5 years before battery degradation becomes noticeable. The batteries in the case last longer since they cycle less. Most premium earbuds retain 80% battery capacity after 500 charge cycles (about 2 years of daily use).

ANC earbuds vs over-ear headphones for noise cancellation?

Over-ear headphones (like the Sony WH-1000XM5) still have better ANC due to the larger ear cup seal. But premium earbuds like the WF-1000XM5 are now 85-90% as effective, and the portability advantage is massive. For commuting and office use, earbuds win. For long-haul flights, over-ear still edges ahead.
